---
title: 'Peer-Mentoring Playbook: Shared Responsibility'
date: 2026-10-08
---


- **Identifier**: Shared Responsibility

- **Problem**: A peer-mentoring group needs to be self-sustaining. _Someone_
  needs to assume responsibility for certain activities, e.g. sending out
  invitations or preparing material. The problem with individuals assuming
  responsibility is twofold. 1) Circumstances change and individuals may no
  longer be able to fill the role. 2) If one individual assumes one
  responsibility over a long period of time, they develop a routine and become
  efficient; at the same time, the other people in the group become used to the
  role being filled by one individual. In consequence, when both circumstances
  appear together, a group might loose a core competence and even cease to
  exist.

- **Objective**: Prevent the group from loosing competence when individual
  circumstances change, and, at the same time, grow competence in all members.

- **Approach**: Establish a routine for assuming shared responsibility among
  all members. Keep a list of all responsibilities that need to be taken care
  of and develop a scheme that allows all members to volunteer for a certain
  period of time. To foster coherence in the group and to lower the barrier of
  entry, have the term of the outgoing volunteer overlap with that of the
  incoming volunteer (which also establishes a backup soulution if individual
  circumstances change).

- **Prerequisites**: A shared space to collaborate on a group's self-governing
  principles and documents.
