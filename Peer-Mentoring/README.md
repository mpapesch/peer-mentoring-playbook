## Peer-Mentoring Skills

This acts as an index directory to the resources available to develop skills
for participating in peer-mentoring groups. For each skill, there should be a
fact-sheet describing its objective, intention and rationale; and/or
Observation, Effect, Need, Request.

- **Content Preparation**: A peer-mentoring group depends on members to present
  both, their current work, and problems and obstacles they face. Guidelines on
  how to prepare content help to improve the value the presenter and the audience
  get from the experience.

- **Giving Feedback**: The opportunitiy for receiving feedback is one of the
  most valuable parts of a peer-mentoring group. However, presenting work or
  obstacles also makes vulnerable. The way of giving feedback has a major impact on
  climate and culture of a peer-mentoring group.
